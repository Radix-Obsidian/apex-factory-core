Four channel-tuned drafts for the **first external demand test** of the factory's power. Generated by

## The locked offer (what these all say)

- **Free kill.** Bring an idea → a brutal, default-to-NO verdict judged against live market data (real named
  competitors, real demand evidence). The NO is the front door.
- **Free first build.** If it survives, we build + ship it *with* you — **free for the first few survivors**
  (we want the case studies), **paid after that**.
- **Audience:** solo founders / vibe-coders who ship with Lovable/v0/Bolt/Cursor but make $0.
- **The proof:** the only credibility anchor is the two true self-kills (the LLM-infra commodity kill + the
  MCPShield double-kill). No invented metrics, no fake testimonials — by design.

## Editorial decisions (resolved 2026-06-17)

1. **No brand name.** These are a personal, first-person build-in-public probe — there's no product, so there's
   no brand. The `[BRAND]` placeholder was removed; the copy reads as the founder offering this directly.
2. **"machine" stays.** It's a machine that runs the line — accurate and punchy. The critic's "sounds productized"
   note was overruled: it's true and it reads well.
3. **"MCPShield" stays named.** It's a killed experiment, not a shipped product. Naming it real (and that the name
   was already taken) is *more* credible than hiding behind "an AI-governance SaaS" — the lesson is the value, not
   the name of the dead experiment.

## What this probe measures (the actual signal)

The point is **not** vanity engagement. Watch two things:

1. **Do strangers accept a NO?** Do people hand over an idea *knowing* the likely answer is "don't build this"?
   If the kill-verdict alone gets takers, the andon-cord positioning is real.
2. **Do they opt into "now build the survivor"?** Of the ideas that survive, how many want the free build?

If people take the NO and walk (no build-attach), the NO is a vitamin and the build is the business — and we
learned it for ~$0. **Log each idea + verdict + whether they came back via the outcome corpus:**
`node recycled/recycled-scan.mjs outcome <slug> <made_money|failed|unknown> [revenue]` — this is the moat seed
(D15) starting to compound on real external ideas, not just our own.
