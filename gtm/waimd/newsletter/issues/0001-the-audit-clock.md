takeaways:
  - "**New Entrant is a status, not a formality.** The day your operating authority activates, you begin an 18-month monitoring period under FMCSA's New Entrant program."
  - "**The Safety Audit usually lands in the first ~12 months.** It reviews your safety-management systems — driver files, drug & alcohol program, hours-of-service records, vehicle maintenance, and insurance."
  - "**It's about systems, not luck.** Auditors check whether the required programs *exist and are documented*, not whether you've had a perfect year on the road."
  - "**Failing has teeth.** A failed audit can lead FMCSA to revoke your new-entrant registration; you generally must fix the findings (a corrective action plan) to keep operating."
  - "**Most failures are avoidable.** The common misses are missing paperwork and programs that were never set up — not catastrophic safety events."

The most dangerous thing about the New Entrant Safety Audit is how quiet it is. Nobody calls to
start a countdown. You get your authority, you start hauling, and the window opens in the
background. This briefing exists so it never surprises you — and the rest of this series walks
the exact programs auditors look for. Verify specifics against FMCSA; this is decision-support,
not legal advice.
