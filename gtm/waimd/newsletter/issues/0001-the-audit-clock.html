The audit clock started the day you got your authority

Every brand-new interstate carrier enters an 18-month FMCSA probation called New Entrant. Inside that window, FMCSA runs a Safety Audit of your operation — and a failed audit can pull the registration you just worked to get. The good news: it's pass/fail on paperwork you control.

 

KEY TAKEAWAYS

1 New Entrant is a status, not a formality. The day your operating authority activates, you begin an 18-month monitoring period under FMCSA's New Entrant program.
2 The Safety Audit usually lands in the first ~12 months. It reviews your safety-management systems — driver files, drug & alcohol program, hours-of-service records, vehicle maintenance, and insurance.
3 It's about systems, not luck. Auditors check whether the required programs *exist and are documented*, not whether you've had a perfect year on the road.
4 Failing has teeth. A failed audit can lead FMCSA to revoke your new-entrant registration; you generally must fix the findings (a corrective action plan) to keep operating.
5 Most failures are avoidable. The common misses are missing paperwork and programs that were never set up — not catastrophic safety events.

▸ DO THIS NOW

Find the date your authority activated and count forward — your audit window is the next ~12 months. Put that range on a calendar today, before anything else competes for it.
