subject: Clearinghouse + drug testing — the program auditors expect on day one
dek: >
  Before any driver performs a safety-sensitive function, FMCSA expects a working drug & alcohol
  testing program and Clearinghouse registration already in place. This is one of the first things
  a Safety Audit checks — and "we were getting to it" is exactly the gap auditors are trained to find.
date: June 25, 2026
issueNo: 2
slug: clearinghouse-before-the-keys
ctaLead: The program has to exist before the first dispatch. Stand it up now, not at audit time.
takeaways:
  - "**The program comes before the driver, not after.** A DOT drug & alcohol testing program must be operational *before* anyone performs a safety-sensitive function — including driving a CMV."
  - "**Most small carriers join a consortium (C/TPA).** A consortium/third-party administrator runs your random testing pool and recordkeeping so a one- or two-truck carrier can meet the same federal standard."
  - "**Register in the FMCSA Clearinghouse.** Carriers must register and query the Clearinghouse for drug & alcohol violations before a driver first operates, and at least once a year for current drivers."
  - "**Pre-employment isn't optional.** A pre-employment drug test (and a Clearinghouse query) is generally required before a new driver's first safety-sensitive function."
  - "**Keep the proof.** Auditors want to see the program, the testing records, and your Clearinghouse queries — not just your word that a pool exists."
doThisNow: If you haven't registered in the FMCSA Clearinghouse and joined a consortium/C-TPA, treat that as your top priority this week — before another driver turns a key.
cfr:
  - 49 CFR Part 382
  - 49 CFR Part 382, Subpart G (Clearinghouse)

Of every item on the audit, this is the one most likely to already be out of compliance the day you
read it — because it's the one that has to be true *before* you start, not after. Set the program up
once and it runs quietly in the background for years. This is decision-support, not legal advice;
confirm the exact testing and query requirements against FMCSA.
